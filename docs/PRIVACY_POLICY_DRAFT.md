# KeyHollow Privacy Policy

**Effective:** August 31, 2026
**Public version:** https://www.keyhollow.com/privacy

KeyHollow is a local-only encrypted photo vault. This policy describes KeyHollow V1.

## Information KeyHollow collects

KeyHollow does not require an account. The app contains no advertising or analytics SDKs and does not send vault contents, passcodes, encryption keys, usage history, or device identifiers to a KeyHollow server.

If you contact support, KeyHollow receives only the information you voluntarily provide, such as your email address, device and app version, and description of the problem. Never send private photos, vault passcodes, or encryption keys to support.

## Photos access and importing

KeyHollow uses Apple's photo-selection and permission interfaces. Selected images are processed on your device, re-encoded to reduce embedded metadata, encrypted locally, and stored in the app's protected container.

**Copy to Vault** keeps the original in Photos. **Move to Vault** encrypts and verifies the vault copy before asking iOS to delete the original. If deletion is denied or cannot be completed for every eligible item, KeyHollow treats the operation as a copy. Apple processes photo-library permissions and changes under its own terms and privacy policy.

## Local storage and security

Vault photos, thumbnails, and manifests use authenticated encryption. Each vault has independent cryptographic key material. Vault passcodes and usable encryption keys are not stored as plaintext files. KeyHollow does not substitute Face ID, Touch ID, or the iPhone device passcode for a vault passcode.

No security system can guarantee protection against every threat. A compromised, modified, or already-unlocked device may weaken these protections.

## Data loss and recovery

KeyHollow has no account-based passcode reset, cloud backup, or synchronization. Its protected vault-data directory is excluded from ordinary device backup. Losing a passcode, deleting a vault or the app, erasing or losing the device, or device failure may permanently eliminate access to vault contents.

## Sharing, sale, and automated training

KeyHollow does not sell personal information, share it for cross-context behavioral advertising, or use private vault contents to train artificial-intelligence systems.

## Children's privacy

KeyHollow is not directed to children under 13 and is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Changes

This policy may change when KeyHollow's features or legal obligations change. The public policy will display its effective date.

# KeyHollow Support — Draft

## About KeyHollow V1

KeyHollow V1 is a local-only encrypted photo vault for iPhone. It does not use accounts, KeyHollow cloud storage, Face ID, Touch ID, or the iPhone device passcode as a substitute for a vault passcode.

## Before contacting support

- Confirm the passcode is between 8 and 20 digits.
- KeyHollow passcodes cannot be recovered or reset without opening the vault with its current passcode.
- A passcode opens only its associated vault; different vaults use different passcodes.
- After too many incorrect attempts, wait for the displayed cooldown to finish.
- **Copy to Vault** keeps the original in Photos.
- **Move to Vault** becomes a copy if iOS does not delete every eligible original.
- Deleting KeyHollow, erasing the device, or deleting a vault can permanently remove access to locally stored vault contents.

## Reporting a problem

Include the KeyHollow version, iOS version, iPhone model, the action you were taking, and any on-screen message. Never send a vault passcode, encryption key, or private photo to support.

## Security reports

Security reports should include clear reproduction steps and the affected version. Do not include real private media or attempt to access another person's device or data.
